# Architecture rules

- Scope Rotina palette and accent styles under `.rotina-theme` so visual changes cannot recolor Invex Gestor.

- Keep payment policies and Rotina pricing on unauthenticated routes with a shared public footer; this lets buyers review purchase conditions without granting access to company data.

- Reuse the existing anamnesis review and PDF-generation flow for completed-anamnesis edits and later prescription additions; this preserves the current clinical permissions and storage behavior.
- Keep required-answer validation shared between review readiness and submission so an empty multi-selection cannot bypass completion checks.
- Keep inactivity exemptions and timeout in the shared session policy consumed by AuthContext so every application screen follows the same logout rule.
- Invex Rotina is a personal product isolated from Invex Gestor: own routine_* tables with per-user RLS, Premium/limits decided in the database, and accounts without a user_roles row are redirected away from Gestor routes; this keeps self-signup users out of company data.
- Premium is decided per product in the database: routine_is_premium (rotina_premium), fitness_is_premium (fitness_premium) and financas_is_premium (financas_premium) each combine an admin grant with an active subscription of their own product, and the payments webhook is the only writer of subscriptions; this keeps paid access server-decided and stops one product unlocking the other.
- Invex Finanças (/financeiro/pessoal) is a personal product with its own login: personal_* tables with per-user RLS, read-only sharing by e-mail, and its palette scoped under `.financas-theme`; this keeps personal money data apart from company finance.
- Only Super Admin (or service-role functions) can set profiles.company_id, enforced by a DB trigger; Invex Gestor routes require a user_roles row with a company, while Fitness/Rotina/Finanças accept any personal account with per-user RLS; this keeps self-signup accounts out of company data.
- Personal accounts (Rotina/Finanças/Fitness) sign up without upfront email confirmation; `personal_email_status()` records proof (email-link session, social login or company role) server-side and the shared EmailConfirmBanner warns, then blocks after the grace period in `emailConfirmPolicy.ts`; this keeps signup instant while still verifying ownership of the address.
- Super Admin manages personal-app accounts in one screen: listing only through the security-definer `admin_personal_accounts()` (fed by `personal_app_usage` via `personal_touch_app`), manual Premium per app through `admin_set_app_premium`, and permanent deletion through the `delete-personal-account` function, which refuses company-linked, protected or actively-subscribed accounts; this shows and manages Rotina/Fitness/Finanças users without exposing their transactions, workouts or tasks.
- Stock changes from Produção and Vendas go only through security-definer RPCs (register_raw_entry/register_production, finalize_sale/cancel_sale for PDV sales with FIFO lot allocation in sale_item_lots, stock guarded by sales.estoque_baixado; save_sale_product/register_product_lot let Vendas work without Logística or Produção; unit fractionality is checked server-side by check_qty_unit); materials.tipo (comum/materia_prima/produto_final) is set by those RPCs and drives the special dashboard cards; this keeps lot balance, stock, payables and cross-module permissions consistent in one transaction.
- Sidebar module sections with sub-sections (Logística, Gestão de Pessoas, Vendas, Financeiro) render through the single `renderSection` in AppSidebar with bold subgroups; this keeps every module's menu identical in look and open/scroll behavior.
- Finance entries created by other modules carry `origem` (`vendas` from PDV, `producao` from raw-material entries) and Contas a Pagar/Receber label and filter by it; this lets finance see where each bill came from without duplicating data.
- Module access is decided by one rule, `resolveModuleAccess` in the UI and `module_is_switched_off` inside `user_can_write_module` on the server: a module switched off for the company (Clínica off turns off its sub-modules) or for the user blocks everyone except Super Admin, admin_empresa included; this keeps Super Admin's switches binding in both the menu and the data.

- Fitness floating records use FitnessPopup with product-scoped theme tokens, and the workout execution/summary screens are full-screen views under `.fitness-workout` tokens that only render state from the single active-session hook (rest countdown derived from `restEndsAt`, no second timer); this keeps one palette for training in any chosen theme without recoloring Gestor or duplicating workout logic.
